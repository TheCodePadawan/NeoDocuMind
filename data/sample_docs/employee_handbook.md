# Northwind Analytics — Employee Handbook (2026)

## 1. Working Hours and Remote Work
Northwind operates on a hybrid model. Employees are expected to be in the office
at least two days per week, typically Tuesday and Thursday. Core collaboration
hours are 10:00–16:00 local time, during which staff should be reachable. Fully
remote arrangements are available for roles approved by a department head and
must be reviewed every six months.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per year, accruing at
2.08 days per month. Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next
calendar year; any balance above 5 days is forfeited on 31 January. PTO requests
should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## 3. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. A doctor's note
is required for any absence longer than three consecutive working days. Unused
sick days do not carry over.

## 4. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave must be
taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

## 5. Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred, with an
itemized receipt for any expense over 25 USD. Meals during business travel are
reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Personal entertainment and alcohol are not
reimbursable. Reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle after approval.

## 6. Professional Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses,
conferences, and books. Unused learning budget does not roll over. Requests are
approved by the employee's direct manager.

## 7. Equipment
New employees receive a company laptop and a 250 USD home-office stipend in their
first month. Hardware is refreshed every three years. Lost or damaged equipment
must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
