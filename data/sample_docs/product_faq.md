# Northwind Insights Platform — Product FAQ

## What is the Northwind Insights Platform?
Northwind Insights is a cloud analytics platform that lets teams connect data
sources, build dashboards, and share automated reports. It supports more than 50
data connectors, including PostgreSQL, Snowflake, and Google BigQuery.

## Pricing Tiers
- **Starter**: 29 USD per user/month. Up to 5 dashboards and daily data refresh.
- **Professional**: 79 USD per user/month. Unlimited dashboards, hourly refresh,
  and email report scheduling.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing. Includes SSO, audit logs, a dedicated success
  manager, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Annual billing receives a 20% discount compared to monthly billing.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Enterprise customers are guaranteed 99.9% monthly uptime. If uptime falls below
this threshold, customers receive service credits: 10% of the monthly fee for
uptime between 99.0% and 99.9%, and 25% for uptime below 99.0%.

## Data Refresh
Starter plans refresh data once per day. Professional plans refresh hourly.
Enterprise plans support near real-time refresh as frequently as every five
minutes.

## Support
Starter and Professional plans include email support with a 24-hour response
target. Enterprise plans include 24/7 priority support with a one-hour response
target for critical issues.

## Data Residency
Customer data can be hosted in the United States, the European Union, or
Australia. The hosting region is selected during onboarding and cannot be changed
afterward without migrating to a new workspace.

## Free Trial
A 14-day free trial of the Professional plan is available with no credit card
required. At the end of the trial, accounts automatically move to the Starter
plan unless an upgrade is purchased.
